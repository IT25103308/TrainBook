Privacy Policy

TrainBook student project · Short summary

We use the details you enter to manage accounts, reservations and tickets in this project demonstration.

  • Account records contain your name, email and phone number. Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable text.
  • Booking records include passenger details, NIC or passport number, contact phone, journey, seats and payment status.
  • Payments use dummy card details. Full card numbers and CVVs are not saved in payment records.
  • Session cookies support sign-in. You can view and edit your profile. Use test personal details for the demonstration.
