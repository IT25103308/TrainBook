TrainBook student project · Short summary

TrainBook is a SLIIT project demonstration, not an official railway booking service.

  • Use test details, keep your login private, and do not misuse another passenger’s account.
  • Seats are held for ten minutes after reservation succeeds. Unpaid holds expire automatically; cancelling a pending reservation releases its seats.
  • Payments are simulated. No real charge is made, and demo tickets are not valid for travel.
  • Check the journey, date, passenger details and seats before confirming. Availability may change until your reservation succeeds.
